Who may use SensQuest

SensQuest is a field-survey tool for authorized adult investigators and supervisors. Your organization supplies your account and survey assignments. Use only your own authorized account and the permissions assigned to your role. Contact your organization if your access is incorrect or you cannot sign in.

Responsible collection

Before collecting a response, explain the survey’s purpose and obtain the person’s consent, including permission for any recording, photograph, or location capture. Follow the survey notice and your organization’s instructions. Do not survey or record minors under the current adults-only policy. Stop collecting if the person refuses or withdraws consent. Do not submit fabricated answers, collect unnecessary personal information, or upload content you are not entitled to use.

Device and offline data

Keep your device secure and report loss or unauthorized access promptly. Responses and media can remain on the device while offline. Check the synchronization status before removing local data. Signing out, clearing local data, or uninstalling the app does not request deletion of records already held by the organization. A factory reset can permanently remove unsent work.

The Privacy Policy explains the data collected, its uses, recipients, and your rights. It is available from sign-in and Profile, including offline. Support reports are optional.
